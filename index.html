Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I upload and export? ▼

AquaPixel Studio supports input decoding of JPEG, PNG, and WebP natively across all modern browsers. You can export your processed image into lossless PNG, highly compatible JPEG, or modern WebP with custom compression levels.

Why does the first AI Background Removal take a few moments? ▼

Upon first use, the browser downloads the quantized IS-Net ONNX neural network weights (~40 MB) from jsDelivr CDN and compiles the WebAssembly runtime. Once downloaded, your browser caches the model, making subsequent background removals instantaneous.

Does upscaling create genuine high-resolution photographic detail? ▼

No. Our high-fidelity resizer uses advanced bicubic interpolation and algorithmic edge sharpening. While this significantly reduces pixelation and edge degradation when scaling up, no interpolation engine can invent true ground-truth pixels that were never recorded by the camera sensor.
